THE VOID
Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 2026

The Void is a voice note app built around one principle: your thoughts belong to you. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can have it permanently deleted.

What we collect

  • Voice transcripts. When you speak into the app, your audio is transcribed on-device via the operating system's speech recognition API. If you choose to save a transcript as a gem, the text is stored in your account.
  • Audio recordings. If your device supports it, a raw audio file may be captured alongside the transcript. Saved audio is uploaded to secure cloud storage tied to your account. Unsaved audio is discarded immediately.
  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and a unique identifier from that provider. We do not receive or store your password.

What we do not collect

  • We do not record audio in the background or when the app is not in use.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or third-party analytics.
  • We do not retain transcripts you choose not to save — they are wiped from memory when the countdown expires.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, camera, or any sensor other than the microphone (when you tap the mic button).

How your data is stored

Saved gems are stored in two places:

  • On your device — encrypted using the platform's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore / macOS Keychain). Only your device can read this data.
  • In the cloud — synced to Supabase, a GDPR-compliant database provider. Your data is protected by row-level security: only you, authenticated with your account, can read or modify your gems. Audio files are stored in an access-controlled private bucket.

Third-party services

  • Supabase (privacy policy) — database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Google Sign-In (privacy policy) — optional authentication method.
  • Sign in with Apple (privacy policy) — optional authentication method.

Speech transcription is handled entirely by the operating system's built-in speech recognition (Apple Speech Framework on iOS/macOS, Android SpeechRecognizer on Android). On Android, the app explicitly requests on-device-only recognition (equivalent to the onDevice: true flag in the Android SpeechRecognizer API), which prevents the audio stream from being routed to remote servers even if the device has a cloud-backed speech model installed. Audio is processed locally; we do not send raw audio to our own servers for transcription.

Data retention

Your gems are retained until you delete them or delete your account. You can delete individual gems at any time from within the app. You can delete your entire account and all associated data — gems and audio files — from the account settings screen. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data at any time. The in-app delete account flow permanently removes all data we hold about you. If you need further assistance exercising these rights, contact us at the address below.

Children

The Void is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, where required by law, notify you in-app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
